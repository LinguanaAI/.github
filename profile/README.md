# LinguanaAI

> **Breaking Language Barriers with AI-Powered Translation**

---

## About Us

**LinguanaAI** is on a mission to democratize access to high-quality translation and voice technology for the world's most linguistically diverse regions. We build multilingual AI systems that specialize in Indian and low-resource languages, enabling people to communicate, learn, and connect — across borders and cultures.

---

## What We Build

- **Translation Models** – Custom fine-tuned models for high-quality translation (text, audio, image)
- **Voice Interpreters** – Real-time voice-to-voice translation tools
- **Chatbots** – Interactive language tutors powered by LLMs
- **PDF & Image Translators** – OCR + TTS + NLP-powered multimodal tools
- **APIs** – Plug-and-play REST endpoints for developers and enterprises
